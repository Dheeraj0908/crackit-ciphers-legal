Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the CrackIt: Famous Ciphers mobile application (the "App", "Service", "Game"), developed and operated by Letterlight ("we", "us", or "our").

By downloading, accessing, or playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection & Local Storage

No Account Registration: CrackIt: Famous Ciphers is designed to operate primarily offline. We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal identifiable details (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number) to play.

Local Device Storage: Your gameplay progress, solved ciphers, win streaks, personal preferences, and in-game statistics are stored locally on your device via secure on-device SQLite databases. This information does not transmit to our servers unless you explicitly sync or back up your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is ad-supported and integrates third-party SDKs provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded video ads).

When you use the Game, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit certain technical data, which may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and unique device characteristics.
  • Coarse Location & Network Data: IP address, general approximate geographic location (country/city), and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, video completion, and performance diagnostics.

For comprehensive details on how Google collects, uses, and protects advertising data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services

3. Consent & Privacy Choices (GDPR, UK & US State Laws)

We respect user privacy rights globally and have implemented Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent framework:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users: Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you will be presented with a clear consent dialogue prior to ad serving, allowing you to choose whether to allow personalized advertisements or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Revoking or Changing Consent: You can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time directly from the in-game Settings screen.
  • California & US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to request information regarding data practices or opt out of targeted advertising by resetting your device Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Children's Privacy

CrackIt: Famous Ciphers is designed for general audiences and word puzzle enthusiasts. The Game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions).

If we discover that personal data has been inadvertently collected from a child under the required age without verified parental consent, we will promptly take steps to delete that information from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Security & Retention

Since puzzle progression and game records are stored locally on your device, you retain full ownership and control over this data. You can completely erase all local application data at any time by clearing the application storage via your device's Application Manager or by uninstalling the Game.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices, new features, or updated legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
